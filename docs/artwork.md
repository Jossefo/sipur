# Sipur artwork

Three original raster illustrations were generated with Codex's built-in image generation tool for this refresh on 2026-11-07. The generated PNG originals were converted to WebP for the app. No existing artist or copyrighted character was requested as a style or identity reference.

The app uses these as curated scene artwork. Additional scene-specific images are procedural SVGs in `illustrator.py`. Creating a story does not make an external AI or image-service request.

## Prompt set

### `static/images/bedtime-world.webp`

Use case: illustration-story. Original hero illustration for a Hebrew children's bedtime story web app. Wide landscape 3:2 children's picture book illustration in hand-painted gouache and textured watercolor on warm ivory paper. A tiny child in soft rust pajamas sits reading an open glowing book on a rounded grassy hill beneath an enormous kind crescent moon; a small fox listens nearby. A miniature world of forest trees, tiny warmly lit houses and floating stars unfurls from the open book into a deep midnight teal sky. Contemporary editorial storybook art, understated faces, layered organic shapes, subtle paper grain, fine colored-pencil details. Muted deep teal, warm ochre, dusty terracotta, soft cream and sage. Comforting bedtime atmosphere. Central subjects, generous breathing room, no text, letters, logos, watermark, mockup, border or UI.

### `static/images/lantern-forest.webp`

Use case: illustration-story. Finished 3:2 landscape forest scene in hand-painted gouache and watercolor with visible ivory paper grain and colored-pencil details. A small child in a terracotta coat, seen from behind with no specific identity, and a gentle fox follow a winding path carrying a softly glowing amber lantern. Rounded sage and deep teal trees frame a distant treehouse with warm windows, stars and a crescent moon. Layered foliage, mushrooms, wildflowers, organic shapes. Midnight teal, sage, ochre, dusty rust, cream. Tender, thoughtful wonder, no fear, no text, letters, logo, watermark, border or UI.

### `static/images/paper-boat.webp`

Use case: illustration-story. Finished 3:2 landscape children's storybook illustration. Hand-painted gouache with textured watercolor on ivory paper, fine colored-pencil edges. A tiny child in rust pajamas and a friendly rabbit sit on a dune by a quiet sea at dusk, watching a paper boat with a warm glowing lantern on calm teal water. Rounded waves, crescent moon, reflected stars, distant hills, grass and wildflowers. Comforting magical realism, midnight teal, sage, terracotta, ochre, cream, organic shapes and paper grain. No text, numbers, branding, watermark, frame or UI.
